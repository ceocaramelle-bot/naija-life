NAIJA LIFE map importer

Fetches real road and building data from OpenStreetMap for one area and makes the district file the game loads.

  1. Find the centre of the area. In Google Maps, press and hold on the spot, then copy the two numbers shown (latitude, longitude).
  2. Enter them below and tap Fetch. Check the preview looks like the place.
  3. Tap Download. Upload the file to your repository as data/districts/abuja-center.json.
